Privacy Policy

1. Purpose

This Privacy Policy explains how the AxeMinersMonitor Monitor mobile application (“the App”), published by Kywalh, handles user data and privacy.

2. Publisher

Publisher: Kywalh Contact Email: kywalh.dev@gmail.com

3. Data Collection by the App

The App does not directly collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data to the publisher. The App is designed to monitor compatible AxeOS-based mining devices configured by the user.

4. Data Stored Locally on the Device

The App may store information locally on the user’s Android device, including: • configured miner addresses or URLs, • optional labels, • optional login credentials entered by the user, • user preferences, • alert settings, • monitoring settings, • cached miner status information, • local logs generated for diagnostic purposes. This information remains on the user’s device unless the user manually shares it, exports it, sends logs, or uses Android or Google services that may back up app data depending on the user’s device settings.

5. Network Communications

The App communicates with mining devices configured by the user, typically through local network or user-configured remote endpoints. The App may send HTTP or HTTPS requests to these devices in order to retrieve monitoring data or perform user-initiated actions. The publisher does not receive or route these communications.

6. Miner and Network Data

Depending on the configured device and firmware, the App may display miner-related technical information such as hashrate, temperature, power usage, firmware version, uptime, shares, pool status, and related operational data. This information is used only inside the App for monitoring, display, alerts, and diagnostics.

7. Credentials

If the user enters login credentials for a configured device, those credentials are stored locally on the Android device for the purpose of connecting to that device. Users are responsible for securing their Android device and for using strong credentials where supported by their mining device or network configuration.

8. Logs and Diagnostics

The App may generate local logs to help diagnose monitoring, network, or device communication issues. Logs may contain technical information such as timestamps, device addresses, request results, error messages, and miner responses. Logs are not automatically sent to the publisher. If the user chooses to share logs, the user is responsible for reviewing their contents before transmission.

9. Google Play and Third-Party Services

The App may be distributed through Google Play. Google may process data independently in connection with app downloads, purchases, payments, crash reporting, security, licensing, analytics, or Play Store account activity. Such processing is governed by Google’s own privacy policies and terms. The publisher does not control Google’s independent data processing.

10. In-App Purchases

If the App offers in-app purchases, payment and purchase processing are handled by Google Play Billing. The publisher does not receive the user’s full payment card details. The App may locally store purchase entitlement status to unlock paid features.

11. Data Sharing

The publisher does not sell user data. The App does not intentionally share personal data with third parties, except where required by the operating system, Google Play services, user action, legal obligations, or technical platform requirements.

12. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for: • securing their Android device, • protecting miner endpoints, • reviewing logs before sharing them, • configuring HTTPS, VPNs, firewalls, reverse proxies, and authentication where appropriate, • understanding that publicly exposed miner APIs may create security risks.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored by the App remains on the user’s device until deleted by the user, cleared through Android settings, overwritten by the App, or removed when the App is uninstalled. Users may delete app data using Android system settings.

14. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children and is not designed to knowingly collect personal data from children.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect legal requirements, Android platform changes, Google Play policy updates, or App functionality changes. Updated versions will be published within the App or through its official distribution page.
